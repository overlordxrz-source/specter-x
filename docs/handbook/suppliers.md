## Routing and follow-up strategy

Prioritize equipment that closes an immediate validation gate: safe reference instruments/leads, a controlled CSI peer, mounting/calibration fixtures and the selected compute/sensors when their receipt is actually confirmed. Consolidate duplicate supplier requests and avoid incompatible variants. Do not accept a free part merely because it expands the bill of materials.

Before any authorized outreach, verify the official page, exact SKU/interface, current contact channel and project eligibility. Ask for itemized equipment and terms: donation/loan/discount, shipping/customs, return obligations, publication/logo/content expectations, technical support and IP/data access. Do not promise positive results or endorsements. No supplier was contacted in this task.

## Sponsorship/receipt record template

For each accepted item record organization, exact SKU/quantity, donation/loan/purchase basis, dated agreement reference, logistics evidence and private receipt location, asset ID, condition, acceptance outcome, return/credit obligations and owner. Keep shipping addresses, tracking IDs, personal contact details and private correspondence in the approved private registry. Public docs can cite redacted receipt evidence and permitted attribution.

A failed or unsuitable donated device remains a failed/unsuitable device in the scientific ledger. Sponsorship does not change its calibration or evidentiary status.
